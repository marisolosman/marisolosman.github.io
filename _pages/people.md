Many of their theses have led to peer-reviewed papers.

## Prospective students

{: style="text-align: justify" }
I am happy to hear from students interested in climate variability and prediction in the Southern Hemisphere, subseasonal to seasonal forecasting, or climate services. Students in Argentina can apply for doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships from CONICET; please get in touch well before the call to discuss a project. You can reach me at osman@cima.fcen.uba.ar.
